Semi-Analytical Multiphysics Framework for Electro-Thermo-Mechanical Response of Functionally-Graded Metal-Matrix Composite Laminates

Md Shah Wali Ullah, Mohammad Abdul Mohaimin, Tonima Islam, Md Nahid Shahriare, S Reaz Ahmed
Functionally Graded Materials (FGMs) Multiphysics Modeling Displacement-Function Approach Electro-Thermo-Mechanical Coupling Finite Difference Method (FDM)
ABSTRACT

Reliable and computationally efficient prediction of the coupled electro-thermo-mechanical response of functionally graded metal-matrix composite (FGMMC) laminates is of great practical importance for the analysis and design of multifunctional load-bearing structures operating under combined electrical and thermal environments. This study develops a scalar displacement-function-based semi-analytical framework for the coupled electrothermo-mechanical analysis of FGMMC laminates subjected to direct-current excitation. The electrical, thermal, and mechanical fields are sequentially coupled by incorporating temperature-dependent electrical and thermal conductivities and Joule heating as an internal heat-generation mechanism. Within the proposed formulation, the thermoelastic response of the laminate is represented by a single scalar displacement function governed by a fourth-order partial differential equation of equilibrium, from which the laminate displacements, strains, and ply-level stresses are systematically recovered. Taylor-series expansions are employed to transform the governing differential equations and associated physical conditions into an equivalent system of algebraic equations, which are solved simultaneously to obtain the field solutions. The framework is demonstrated using symmetric Al-SiC FGMMC laminates with a power-law variation of SiC reinforcement volume fraction through the thickness. A clamped laminate subjected to electrothermal loading is first considered to establish the coupled electrical, thermal, and mechanical responses, followed by a more complex variable mixed-boundary-value problem involving localized current injection, thermal and mechanical loading, and discrete mechanical supports. Parametric analyses are performed to examine the effects of material gradation and electrothermal loading conditions on the electrical potential, temperature distribution, and stress response of the laminates. Excellent agreement with finiteelement simulations confirms the accuracy and appropriateness of the proposed framework. Owing to its modular architecture and computational efficiency, the proposed scheme provides a versatile platform for analyzing and designing electrically activated functionally graded laminated structures under complex physical conditions, and can be readily extended to more general coupled multiphysics problems with minimal modification.

Assessment of Landfill Gas-to-Energy Potential and Technologies at Arefin Nagar and Fatehabad Landfills Using Site-Specific LandGEM Modeling

8th International Conference on Chemical Engineering (ICCHE 2026), BUET
Pritam Biswangri, Ayan Chowdhury, Amitav Mandal, Mohammad Abdul Mohaimin
Landfill Gas-to-Energy USEPA LandGEM v3.02 Methane Emissions Modeling Anaerobic Digestion & IC Engines Sustainable Waste Management
ABSTRACT

The second-largest city in Bangladesh, Chattogram, produces over 3,000 tonnes of municipal solid waste (MSW) every day. The majority of this trash is dumped in open dumpsites at Arefin Nagar and Anandabazar without leachate treatment or gas control systems. Since organic waste makes up 62-73% of the MSW stream, anaerobic decomposition produces large amounts of landfill gas (LFG) that are now released uncontrollably into the atmosphere, posing both greenhouse gas and public health risks to nearby communities. As Chattogram City Corporation prepares to transition disposal operations to a new engineered landfill at Fatehabad by 2027, a critical opportunity exists to integrate gas-to-energy infrastructure into both the closing and incoming sites. This study projects methane generation at Arefin Nagar and Fatehabad through 2090 using the USEPA LandGEM v3.02 Site Specific-1 (SP-1) model calibrated with locally determined parameters. According to modeling data, peak methane generation was 19,647 Mg yr⁻¹ at Arefin Nagar (2027) and 22,600 Mg yr⁻¹ at Fatehabad (2038). From 2030 to 2041, the overall generation exceeded 20,000 Mg yr⁻¹. Converting this LFG to electricity via reciprocating IC engines yields peak recoverable outputs of 71.7 GWh/yr and 82.5 GWh/yr at the two sites, respectively, rising to over 130 GWh/yr under combined heat-and-power (CHP) operation at higher collection efficiency.
