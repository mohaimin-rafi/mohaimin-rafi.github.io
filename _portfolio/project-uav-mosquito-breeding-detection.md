### Overview
Designed, fabricated, and field-tested a custom quadcopter UAV integrated with an onboard camera, GPS telemetry, and a deep-learning computer-vision system for autonomous detection of potential mosquito breeding sites.

### Key Highlights & Technical Achievements
* **UAV Hardware Integration:** Designed and assembled a functional UAV platform using flight controllers, BLDC motors, ESCs, telemetry systems, and transmitter-receiver modules.
* **Aerial Acquisition Pipeline:** Developed an automated aerial image-acquisition pipeline from UAV video feeds for real-time environmental monitoring.
* **Deep Learning Object Detection:** Trained and implemented a YOLOv5 object-detection model on a custom dataset to identify stagnant water and potential breeding sites in difficult-to-access urban locations.
* **Geospatial Logging:** Integrated GPS telemetry to geolocate and log identified breeding sites, enabling targeted intervention and more efficient public-health surveillance.
